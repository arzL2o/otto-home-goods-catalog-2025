# OTTO Home Goods 2025 Catalog Images

Per-variant product images extracted from the official OTTO Home Goods 2025 product catalog PDF.

- 172 product variants across vanities (Signature, Essentials, Floating), mirrors, quartz tops, and sinks
- 159 unique product photos (some variants share a marketing photo)
- Filenames follow `<Series>_<Color>_<Size>[_<Sub>]_p<Page>_x<XRef>.jpg`

Used by the companion CSV to provide direct image URLs for each variant row.
